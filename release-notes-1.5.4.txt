What's new in 1.5.4:

• New illustrations on the intro screens
• Signing back in restores your name, avatar and contacts — they used to come back empty
• The activity feed finally records what happens in an event
• Fixed profile photo upload, which used to fail with an error
• Switching language now applies across the whole app at once
• Delete chat messages and edit budget categories
• Changes save offline, and a refused change is now visible
• Signing out stops notifications for that account on the device
• Dietary preferences are shared only within the event they belong to
• The restore purchases button is finally labelled for what it does
